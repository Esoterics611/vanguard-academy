# Part VI: Strategic Advisory

> **Strategic advisory is the highest-value, most intellectually demanding function in investment banking. The first practitioners will define the standards and methodologies that anchor the industry for decades.**

---

## Overview

Strategic advisory requires the synthesis of financial analysis, competitive intelligence, organizational insight, and market judgment into recommendations that shape a protocol's future direction. In the DAO context, the strategic advisor must not only develop sound strategy but communicate it persuasively to a distributed stakeholder base that will ultimately vote on whether to adopt it.

This part of the handbook provides the **complete strategic advisory toolkit** — from competitive analysis frameworks through market positioning, growth strategy, go-to-market planning, and the professional delivery of strategic advisory engagements. It concludes with the course conclusion, connecting the full arc of the handbook.
