# Valuation Model Templates

The following template structures should be used for the valuation and financial modeling exercises throughout this handbook. Detailed Excel/spreadsheet implementations are available separately.

---

## Template 1: Protocol DCF Model

**Inputs:**
- Historical revenue (12-24 months)
- Revenue drivers by source
- Operating expense breakdown
- Token supply schedule
- Discount rate assumptions

**Forecast:**
- 5-year annual projections of revenue, expenses, PFCF
- Quarterly granularity for Year 1

**Terminal Value:**
- Both perpetuity growth and exit multiple methods

**Output:**
- Intrinsic protocol value
- Per-token value
- Sensitivity tables
- Scenario analysis (bull/base/bear)

---

## Template 2: Comparable Company Analysis

**Inputs:**
- Peer group identification (5-10 protocols)
- Financial metrics for each peer (revenue, TVL, users, growth rate)

**Multiples:**
- P/R, P/E (where calculable), EV/R, Market Cap/TVL, Revenue/TVL

**Output:**
- Implied valuation range for subject protocol
- Premium/discount analysis

---

## Template 3: M&A Transaction Model

**Inputs:**
- Standalone valuations of acquirer and target
- Synergy projections
- Transaction terms (exchange ratio, consideration mix, lock-up)

**Analysis:**
- Accretion/dilution analysis
- Pro forma ownership
- Synergy realization timeline

**Output:**
- Recommended exchange ratio with sensitivity analysis
- Fairness assessment

---

## Template 4: Token Supply Model

**Inputs:**
- Genesis supply
- Allocation by category
- Vesting schedules
- Emission schedule
- Burn projections

**Output:**
- Monthly circulating supply over 5 years
- Annual inflation/deflation rate
- Insider ownership trajectory
- Treasury depletion timeline

---

## Template 5: Treasury Management Model

**Inputs:**
- Current treasury composition
- Monthly burn rate
- Revenue projections
- Asset allocation targets

**Output:**
- Runway analysis
- Monthly treasury balance projections
- Stress test results
- Risk metrics (VaR, concentration)
