# Capstone Case Study: NexusDAO Comprehensive Advisory Engagement

This capstone exercise integrates every skill and framework developed throughout the handbook. Completing it at a professional level demonstrates readiness to practice DAO investment banking.

---

## Scenario

You are a Vanguard DAO Academy graduate hired as the lead advisor to **NexusDAO**, a mid-tier DeFi lending protocol preparing for a strategic transformation.

### Protocol Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Protocol** | Lending and borrowing across Ethereum and Arbitrum |
| **TVL** | $400 million |
| **Annual Revenue** | $18 million |
| **Treasury** | $25 million USDC, $10 million ETH, 800 million NEXUS tokens (FDV at $1.00 = $1 billion notional) |
| **Monthly Burn Rate** | $2.5 million |
| **Team** | 25 core contributors |
| **Governance** | Active but fragmented, with 3 competing delegate factions |

### Recent Events

1. A smaller competitor (**MicroLend**) has approached NexusDAO about a potential merger
2. A large Layer 2 (**ChainX**) has offered a **$5 million deployment grant** to bring NexusDAO to their chain

---

## Deliverable Format

Present your work as a **complete Strategic Pitch Book** suitable for presentation to NexusDAO's governance community.

### Pitch Book Structure

1. **Cover Page** — Protocol name, engagement title, date
2. **Executive Summary** — One-page key findings and recommendations
3. **Valuation Analysis** — DCF, comparable, precedent transaction results
4. **M&A Assessment** — MicroLend merger evaluation with recommendation
5. **Strategic Assessment** — ChainX deployment evaluation with recommendation
6. **Capital Raising Plan** — Instrument selection, terms, investor targeting
7. **Treasury Strategy** — Three-Bucket allocation, diversification plan, risk framework
8. **Financial Impact Analysis** — Pro forma projections under recommended strategy
9. **Risk Analysis** — Key risks and mitigations across all recommendations
10. **Implementation Roadmap** — Phased timeline with milestones and governance requirements

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Professional Standard**

This exercise should take 20-30 hours to complete at a professional level. Each section should contain the depth and rigor you would expect from a presentation to a DAO governance community that will vote on whether to adopt your recommendations.
{% endhint %}
