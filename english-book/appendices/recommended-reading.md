# Recommended Reading

Supplementary resources for deepening your understanding of the concepts in this handbook.

---

## Foundational Finance

**Valuation & Corporate Finance:**
- Aswath Damodaran, *Investment Valuation* — the definitive guide to valuation frameworks. Essential for adapting DCF and comparable analysis to DAOs.
- Berk & DeMarzo, *Corporate Finance* — comprehensive corporate finance textbook covering capital structure, payout policy, and M&A.
- McKinsey & Company, *Valuation: Measuring and Managing the Value of Companies* — practitioner-oriented valuation methodology.

**Investment Banking:**
- Joshua Rosenbaum & Joshua Pearl, *Investment Banking: Valuation, LBOs, M&A, and IPOs* — the standard IB training manual. Many frameworks in this handbook are adapted from Rosenbaum & Pearl.

**Ethics & Professional Standards:**
- CFA Institute, *Standards of Practice Handbook* — the ethical framework adapted in Chapter 1.

---

## DeFi & Crypto Economics

**Protocol Analysis:**
- Campbell Harvey, Ashwin Ramachandran, Joey Santoro, *DeFi and the Future of Finance* — academic treatment of DeFi protocols and their economic mechanics.

**Tokenomics:**
- Yos Riady, *Tokenomics* — practical guide to token design, distribution, and economics.

**Network Value Theory:**
- Chris Burniske, Jack Tatar, *Cryptoassets: The Innovative Investor's Guide* — introduces the quantity theory of money (MV=PQ) applied to crypto assets.

---

## Key Research Papers

- **Alabi (2017)** — "Digital Token Valuation" — early framework for applying Metcalfe's Law to crypto network valuation.
- **Peterson (2018)** — "Metcalfe's Law as a Model for Bitcoin's Value" — empirical analysis of network value models.
- **Xu, Chen, & Kou (2019)** — "A Systematic Review of DeFi Protocols" — comprehensive survey of protocol categories and mechanisms.

---

## Data & Analytics

- **Dune Analytics Documentation** — [dune.com/docs](https://dune.com/docs) — essential for learning the SQL query syntax and available data tables.
- **DefiLlama API Documentation** — programmatic access to TVL, revenue, and fee data.
- **Electric Capital Developer Report** — annual survey of developer activity across crypto ecosystems. Key source for developer metrics in competitive analysis.

---

## Governance & Mechanism Design

- **Vitalik Buterin** — "Moving Beyond Coin Voting Governance" (2021) — foundational critique of token-weighted governance and exploration of alternatives.
- **Vitalik Buterin** — Various research posts on governance, mechanism design, and Ethereum economics — vitalik.eth.limo
- **Tim Roughgarden** — *Foundations and Frontiers of Blockchain Research* (lectures) — academic rigor applied to mechanism design.
- **BlockScience** — cadCAD documentation and tokenomics research — agent-based simulation frameworks.
- **Optimism Collective** — Governance documentation and design principles — innovative two-house governance model.
- **a16z crypto** — Governance research and frameworks — practical governance design resources.

## Industry Data & Analysis

- **Messari** — Research reports and data analytics
- **The Block Research** — Industry analysis and quantitative data
- **Placeholder VC** — Research on crypto network valuation frameworks

## Legal & Regulatory

- **FATF Updated Guidance for a Risk-Based Approach to Virtual Assets and VASPs (2021)** — the global standard for AML/KYC in crypto.
- **CFTC v. Ooki DAO (2022)** — landmark case establishing DAO liability as unincorporated associations.
- **EU MiCA Regulation** — comprehensive European framework for crypto-asset regulation.
- **Wyoming DAO LLC Act** — pioneering US state-level DAO legal framework.
