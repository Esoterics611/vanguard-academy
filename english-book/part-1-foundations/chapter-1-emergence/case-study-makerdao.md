MakerDAO provides the foundational case study for understanding DAOs as economic entities requiring investment banking services. Its history illustrates nearly every category of IB activity this course covers.

## How It Works

Users generate the DAI stablecoin by depositing collateral into Maker Vaults. The protocol earns revenue through stability fees (interest on borrowed DAI) and liquidation penalties. MKR token holders govern the protocol and serve as the backstop of last resort — if the system becomes undercollateralized, new MKR tokens are minted and sold to recapitalize the protocol.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Key Point**: MakerDAO's revenue figure ($200M+ annualized) places it in the range of a mid-cap traditional financial services company. This is not a toy — it is a real business requiring real financial advisory.
{% endhint %}

## Investment Banking Activities Illustrated

### Valuation

MKR was valued by the market at various points between $500 million and $3 billion. Fundamental valuation requires modeling:
- DAI supply growth
- Stability fee revenue across collateral types
- Collateral mix evolution (especially the shift to Real World Assets)
- The unique "burn-and-mint" token economics

These valuation techniques are covered in **Part II**.

### Strategic Transformation

The "Endgame" plan proposed by founder Rune Christensen in 2022 was, in investment banking terms, a **comprehensive strategic restructuring**:
- Creation of "SubDAOs" (analogous to spin-off subsidiaries)
- Rebranding (SKY replacing MKR, USDS replacing DAI)
- Fundamental repositioning of competitive strategy

An investment bank advising on this transformation would have conducted competitive analysis, valued the constituent parts, structured the organizational redesign, and managed stakeholder communications — exactly the services covered in **Part VI**.

### Capital Allocation

MakerDAO's decision to allocate over **$1 billion** of its reserves to U.S. Treasury bills through entities like BlockTower and Monetalis was a major treasury management decision requiring:
- Counterparty risk assessment
- Yield optimization
- Liquidity management
- Regulatory considerations

This type of analysis is covered in **Part V**.

### M&A Activity

The SubDAO structure effectively created entities that could acquire specialized protocol functions. The creation of **Spark Protocol** (a lending market forked from Aave) as a SubDAO represented an internal "acquisition" — building rather than buying, but with the same strategic logic of vertical integration that an investment banker would analyze.

### Governance as Stakeholder Management

Every major MakerDAO decision required navigating the preferences of:
- MKR holders (governance power)
- DAI holders (stability concerns)
- Vault users (fee sensitivity)
- Core unit contributors (operational continuity)
- The broader DeFi ecosystem (composability and trust)

This stakeholder management function is precisely what investor relations and strategic advisory services provide in traditional finance.
