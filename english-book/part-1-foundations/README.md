# Part I: Foundations of Decentralized Investment Banking

---

## What This Part Covers

Part I establishes the intellectual and analytical foundations for the entire handbook. Before we can value protocols, advise on mergers, or manage treasuries, we need to answer three fundamental questions:

1. **What are DAOs, really?** Not the oversimplified "a group that votes" definition — but a precise economic and organizational characterization that supports rigorous financial analysis.

2. **What does investment banking look like for DAOs?** How do traditional IB functions — advisory, underwriting, trading, research, asset management, restructuring — translate to the decentralized context?

3. **How do we analyze DAOs financially?** How do you construct financial statements when there's no 10-K filing? What metrics matter? Where does the data come from?

## Prerequisites

- Basic understanding of financial statements
- Familiarity with blockchain concepts (wallets, tokens, smart contracts)
- No prior IB experience required

## Key Takeaways (Preview)

By the end of Part I, you will understand that:

1. DAOs are real economic entities generating billions in revenue and managing tens of billions in assets
2. Each traditional IB function has a direct DAO equivalent
3. DAO financial analysis requires constructing statements from on-chain and off-chain data
4. Professional and ethical standards apply fully — adapted, not abandoned
5. The DAO IB market represents a multi-billion dollar fee opportunity for first movers
