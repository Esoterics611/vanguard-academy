## Why This Chapter Matters

Traditional corporations publish standardized financial statements prepared according to GAAP or IFRS standards. DAOs have no such standardized reporting requirements. But the underlying economic realities these statements capture still exist and must be analyzed.

The ability to construct financial statements from raw on-chain data — and to extract meaningful analytical insights from them — is the foundational skill of the DAO investment banker. Every valuation, every advisory engagement, every strategic recommendation in this handbook builds on the frameworks established in this chapter.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Think About It**

If you were analyzing a traditional company, you'd start with its 10-K filing. Where do you start with a DAO? There's no single document to reference — the analyst must synthesize data from blockchain explorers, governance forums, analytics dashboards, and treasury wallets into a coherent financial picture. This is both the challenge and the competitive advantage of the skilled DAO analyst.
{% endhint %}
