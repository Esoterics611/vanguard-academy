# 2.2 Key Performance Indicators for DAO Analysis

{% hint style="info" %}
**Learning Objectives**

After completing this section, you will be able to:
- Identify and calculate the key protocol activity metrics
- Track and interpret token and governance health metrics
- Assess competitive positioning using market and ecosystem metrics
- Explain the limitations of TVL as a standalone measure
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Key Terms**

- **TVL (Total Value Locked)**: Total value of assets deposited in a protocol's smart contracts
- **Token Velocity**: The rate at which tokens change hands — high velocity may indicate weak governance participation
- **Token Float**: The percentage of total token supply that is freely tradable (not locked)
- **Gini Coefficient**: A measure of distribution inequality; applied to token holdings to assess governance centralization
- **Capital Efficiency**: Revenue generated per dollar of capital deployed (Revenue / TVL)
{% endhint %}

---

Beyond financial statements, DAO analysis requires a set of KPIs that capture protocol health, growth trajectory, and competitive positioning. These are the DAO equivalents of the metrics equity research analysts track for traditional companies.

## 2.2.1 Protocol Activity Metrics

### Total Value Locked (TVL)

TVL is the most widely cited DeFi metric — and the most misunderstood.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Common Mistake: Treating TVL as the Definitive Measure**

TVL has significant limitations:
1. **Inflatable** via recursive borrowing/lending (deposit → borrow → redeposit → repeat)
2. **Incentive-driven** deposits leave when rewards end ("mercenary capital")
3. **Double-counted** across protocols (same dollar counted in Protocol A and Protocol B)
4. **Composition-blind** ($1B in stablecoins ≠ $1B in volatile altcoins)
5. **Revenue-agnostic** — high TVL with zero fees generates no value

The sophisticated analyst adjusts TVL for these factors and tracks **TVL composition** rather than headline TVL alone.
{% endhint %}

### Protocol Revenue

Total fees generated by the protocol's economic activity. Track daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly with year-over-year comparisons.

**Data Sources:** Token Terminal, DefiLlama, Dune Analytics

### Revenue per TVL (Capital Efficiency)

{% hint style="info" %}
**Formula**

`Capital Efficiency = Annual Protocol Revenue / TVL`

Higher ratios indicate superior capital efficiency. This is a marker of protocol quality.

**Example:** If Protocol A has $1B TVL and $100M revenue (10% efficiency) while Protocol B has $1B TVL and $10M revenue (1% efficiency), Protocol A generates 10x more value per unit of capital — despite identical TVL.
{% endhint %}

### Daily Active Users (DAU)

Unique wallet addresses interacting with the protocol per day. Limitations: one person can use multiple wallets, bot activity inflates numbers. But **directional trends** are meaningful.

### Transaction Volume

Total value of transactions processed. For DEXs = trading volume; lending protocols = borrow volume; stablecoins = transfer volume.

## 2.2.2 Token and Governance Metrics

| Metric | What It Measures | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Token Velocity** | Rate of token turnover | High velocity = weak governance participation (tokens traded, not used for voting) |
| **Governance Participation Rate** | % of supply voting on proposals | Low rates (< 10% is common) raise legitimacy and attack vector concerns |
| **Token Holder Distribution** | Concentration of holdings (Gini coefficient) | High concentration = governance centralization risk |
| **Token Float** | % of supply freely tradable | Low float = future dilution pressure as locked tokens unlock |
| **Vesting Schedule Impact** | Timeline and magnitude of unlocks | Large unlock events create selling pressure |

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Think About It**

A protocol has a governance participation rate of 3% and its top 5 wallets control 60% of the token supply. What does this tell you about governance quality? What attack vectors does it create? How would this factor into your investment banking advisory?
{% endhint %}

## 2.2.3 Competitive and Market Position Metrics

**Market Share** — the protocol's share of total activity in its category. Uniswap's share of DEX volume, Aave's share of lending TVL, Lido's share of liquid staking. Market share trends are among the most important indicators of competitive health.

**Switching Costs and Retention** — how easily users can move to competitors, and how well the protocol retains users. Protocols with high switching costs (deep liquidity, established integrations, strong brand) have more defensible positions.

**Developer Activity** — GitHub commit frequency, number of active developers, pace of new feature deployment. Declining developer activity signals loss of innovation capacity.

**Integration Density** — the number and quality of other protocols that integrate with the subject protocol. Deep integration creates network effects and switching costs that enhance competitive positioning.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Key Point**: No single metric tells the full story. The skilled analyst combines activity metrics, token metrics, and competitive metrics into a holistic assessment. The protocol deep dive framework (Section 2.4) provides the structure for this integration.
{% endhint %}

## Section Summary

- TVL is the most cited but most limited DeFi metric — always supplement with revenue and capital efficiency measures
- Protocol revenue and capital efficiency (revenue/TVL) are the strongest indicators of protocol economic quality
- Token and governance metrics reveal centralization risks, dilution pressure, and governance legitimacy
- Competitive metrics (market share, switching costs, developer activity, integration density) assess defensibility
- Combine all three metric categories for a holistic protocol assessment
