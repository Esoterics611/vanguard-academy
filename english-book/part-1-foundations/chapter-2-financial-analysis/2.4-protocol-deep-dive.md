# 2.4 Building the Analytical Foundation: The Protocol Deep Dive

{% hint style="info" %}
**Learning Objectives**

After completing this section, you will be able to:
- Execute a comprehensive 7-section protocol deep dive
- Identify the data sources for each section of the analysis
- Produce a deep dive document suitable for professional IB use
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Key Terms**

- **Protocol Deep Dive**: A comprehensive analysis establishing the factual foundation for all subsequent advisory work — analogous to the company/industry analysis in a traditional IB pitch book
- **Competitive Moat**: A sustainable competitive advantage that protects a protocol from competitors (network effects, switching costs, brand, technology)
{% endhint %}

---

Every engagement in DAO investment banking begins with a **protocol deep dive** — a comprehensive analysis that establishes the factual foundation for all subsequent advisory work. This is analogous to the industry and company analysis that forms the backbone of traditional investment banking pitch books.

## The Protocol Deep Dive Template

### Section 1: Protocol Overview

| Question | What You're Looking For |
|----------|----------------------|
| What does the protocol do? | Core value proposition in one sentence |
| What problem does it solve? | The pain point or inefficiency addressed |
| What is the core economic mechanism? | How value is created and captured |
| Who founded it and when? | Team credibility, track record, decentralization trajectory |
| What is the current development status? | Mainnet maturity, feature completeness, roadmap |

### Section 2: Smart Contract Architecture

| Question | What You're Looking For |
|----------|----------------------|
| What is the high-level technical architecture? | How the pieces fit together |
| What are the key smart contracts and their functions? | Which contracts handle what economic activity |
| Is the protocol upgradeable? | Who controls upgrades — governance, multi-sig, or immutable |
| What is the audit history? | How many audits, by whom, what was found |
| Are there known vulnerabilities? | Outstanding risks, bug bounty program |

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Common Mistake**: Skipping smart contract analysis because "it's a technical thing." As a DAO investment banker, you don't need to audit the code — but you must understand the *architecture* well enough to assess upgradeability risk, governance control surfaces, and the economic logic encoded in the contracts.
{% endhint %}

### Section 3: Tokenomics

- Token supply schedule (initial distribution, vesting, inflation/deflation)
- Token utility (governance, staking, fee capture, collateral)
- Token holder distribution and concentration analysis
- Historical price performance and correlation analysis

### Section 4: Financial Analysis

- Income statement construction (trailing twelve months)
- Balance sheet analysis with treasury composition
- Cash flow analysis and runway calculation
- Key financial metrics and trend analysis

*This section applies everything from [Section 2.1](2.1-dao-financial-statements.md).*

### Section 5: Governance Analysis

- Governance mechanism (token voting, delegation, multi-sig, optimistic governance)
- Governance participation rates and trends
- Key governance decisions and their outcomes
- Governance risk assessment (concentration, attack vectors, voter apathy)

### Section 6: Competitive Analysis

- Market definition and sizing
- Competitor identification and comparison
- Competitive advantages and vulnerabilities
- Market share trends and trajectory

### Section 7: Risk Assessment

| Risk Category | What to Assess |
|--------------|----------------|
| **Smart Contract Risk** | Audit findings, complexity, upgradeability, exploit history |
| **Regulatory Risk** | Jurisdiction, token classification, enforcement exposure |
| **Market Risk** | Liquidity depth, correlation, volatility, concentration |
| **Governance Risk** | Centralization, capture potential, voter apathy |
| **Operational Risk** | Key person dependency, infrastructure fragility, bus factor |

{% hint style="success" %}
**Key Point**: The protocol deep dive is not an academic exercise — it is the foundation of every advisory deliverable. A valuation without a deep dive is speculation. An M&A recommendation without a deep dive is malpractice. Every claim you make to a governance community must be traceable back to the rigorous analysis in your deep dive.
{% endhint %}

## Section Summary

- Every DAO IB engagement begins with a 7-section protocol deep dive
- The deep dive covers: overview, smart contracts, tokenomics, financials, governance, competition, and risks
- Each section has specific data sources and analytical methods
- The deep dive is the factual foundation for all advisory work — valuations, M&A recommendations, and strategic advice
- If you cannot construct a comprehensive deep dive, you are not yet ready for the advisory work that follows
