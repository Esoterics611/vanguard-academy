# Chapter 14: Market Making, Exchange Listings, and Launch Execution

Market making ensures orderly price discovery and trading liquidity. Exchange listings provide access to the largest pools of trading volume. Launch execution coordinates the operational complexity of a TGE across multiple venues and stakeholders — all while navigating a complex regulatory landscape.

## Chapter Overview

This chapter covers the final TGE workstreams:

- **Market maker selection and agreements** — token loan vs. retainer models, red flags, and DEX strategy
- **Exchange listing strategy** — Tier 1/2 prioritization, requirements, and timelines
- **Regulatory considerations** — Howey test analysis, international landscape, geographic restrictions
- **Launch communication and execution** — pre-launch through T+1 week playbook

## Key Concepts

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Market Maker Red Flags**

The crypto market making industry has been plagued by conflicts of interest and manipulative practices. The investment banker must vet market makers carefully and negotiate agreements that protect the protocol from hidden costs — particularly call options and excessive token loans.
{% endhint %}
