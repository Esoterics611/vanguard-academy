# 11.2 TGE Readiness Assessment

Before engaging in TGE advisory, the investment banker must assess whether the protocol is ready for a token launch. Premature launches are among the most common and most damaging mistakes in the crypto ecosystem.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Learning Objectives**

After completing this section, you will be able to:
- Apply the TGE readiness checklist across five dimensions
- Identify specific metric thresholds for readiness by protocol category
- Explain why premature launches are destructive
- Analyze Optimism's staged approach as a model for disciplined timing
{% endhint %}

---

## 11.2.1 The TGE Readiness Checklist

### 1. Product-Market Fit

Does the protocol have a functioning product with genuine, non-incentivized user demand? A protocol that has not demonstrated product-market fit should not launch a token.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Why Premature Launches Destroy Value**

Launching a token before product-market fit creates a liability: the protocol must now manage token price expectations, governance demands, and community communication overhead — in addition to the already-demanding work of finding product-market fit.
{% endhint %}

**Metric thresholds for readiness** (indicative, varies by category):

| Protocol Type | Readiness Threshold |
|--------------|-------------------|
| DEX | $50M+ daily organic volume |
| Lending Protocol | $100M+ total borrows, 50%+ utilization |
| Infrastructure | 100+ integrating protocols |
| Stablecoin | $100M+ circulating supply with peg stability |
| Layer 2 / Chain | $500M+ TVL, 100+ deployed applications |

### 2. Technical Maturity

Has the protocol undergone comprehensive security audits? Is the codebase stable?

**Minimum requirements:**
- At least **two independent audits** from reputable firms covering all deployed contracts
- All Critical and High findings resolved
- Formal verification of critical components (token contract, governance, staking) strongly recommended

Launching a token for a protocol with unresolved security issues is catastrophic — the token attracts attention, attention attracts attackers, and a post-launch exploit destroys both value and reputation.

### 3. Team Stability

Is the core team committed and stable? A TGE during key departures is a red flag. The team must be prepared for the significant increase in obligations: governance management, community communication, financial reporting, regulatory compliance, and market relations.

### 4. Legal Readiness

- Appropriate legal structure established
- Legal counsel has reviewed tokenomics and distribution for regulatory compliance
- Team members' personal legal situations clean (no pending enforcement actions, unresolved tax obligations, or prior crypto regulatory issues)

### 5. Community Readiness

A token launched to an empty room fails.

**Minimum indicators:**
- 10,000+ Discord/Telegram members with 10%+ daily active rate
- Active governance forum with regular discussion
- Organic social media presence with genuine engagement (not bot-driven)

### 6. Treasury Runway

Does the protocol have sufficient **non-token funding** to operate for 18-24 months post-TGE without selling tokens? A protocol forced to sell its own tokens to fund operations creates persistent selling pressure that undermines market performance.

## 11.2.2 Case Study: Optimism's Staged Approach

Optimism, an Ethereum Layer 2 scaling solution, provides an instructive case study in thoughtful TGE timing.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Optimism's Timeline**

**Mainnet launch:** January 2021
**TGE:** May 2022 — a **16-month gap**

During those 16 months, Optimism:
- Achieved significant technical maturity (multiple upgrades, comprehensive testing)
- Built genuine adoption ($500M+ TVL, hundreds of deployed apps)
- Established a strong community through governance discussions and contributor engagement
- Designed a sophisticated governance framework (the Optimism Collective — bicameral Token House and Citizens' House)
- Developed a novel distribution model (airdrop to genuine users with Sybil filtering)
{% endhint %}

**Result:** One of the most successful TGEs of 2022 — achieving broad distribution, strong governance participation, and relatively stable price performance despite launching during a severe bear market.

**Contrast:** Numerous protocols that launched tokens in early 2021 at peak market exuberance, without adequate product-market fit or community development, subsequently saw **90%+ token price declines** as both market conditions deteriorated and the protocols failed to deliver.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Six readiness dimensions must be satisfied before launching a TGE: product-market fit, technical maturity, team stability, legal readiness, community engagement, and treasury runway
- Each dimension has specific, measurable threshold indicators
- Premature launches create liabilities that compound the difficulty of building the protocol
- Optimism's 16-month gap between mainnet and TGE demonstrates the value of disciplined timing
- The investment banker's first advisory duty is honest assessment of readiness — recommending delay when the protocol is not ready
