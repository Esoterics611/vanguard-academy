# Chapter 12: Tokenomics Design

Tokenomics — the economic design of a protocol's token — is the single most important design decision in a TGE. It determines supply dynamics, value accrual, governance characteristics, and long-term economic sustainability. It is the protocol's capital structure, dividend policy, employee compensation plan, and incentive system, all encoded in smart contract logic.

## Chapter Overview

This chapter provides the complete tokenomics design framework:

- **Token supply design** — fixed, inflationary, deflationary, and dynamic models
- **Token allocation** — stakeholder categories, benchmarks, and the insider allocation debate
- **Value accrual mechanisms** — practical design for fee distribution, buyback-burn, ve-models, and staking
- **Governance token design** — voting, delegation, quorum, emergency mechanisms
- **Tokenomics modeling** — supply projections, price impact simulation, and agent-based testing

## Key Concepts
