# 12.3 Tokenomics Modeling and Simulation

Before finalizing tokenomics, the investment banker should construct comprehensive models projecting token supply, implied value, and system behavior under different scenarios. This section covers the three modeling approaches: supply projection, price impact analysis, and agent-based simulation.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Learning Objectives**

After completing this section, you will be able to:
- Build a 5-10 year token supply projection model
- Conduct price impact analysis relating revenue growth to supply expansion
- Understand agent-based simulation frameworks for stress-testing tokenomics
{% endhint %}

---

## 12.5.1 The Token Supply Model

Construct a comprehensive projection of circulating supply over a 5-10 year horizon.

### Model Inputs

| Category | Data Required |
|----------|--------------|
| **Genesis** | Total supply, initial circulating % |
| **Team vesting** | Monthly token releases per vesting schedule |
| **Investor vesting** | Monthly releases per investor schedule |
| **Community emissions** | Monthly rewards distribution schedule |
| **Treasury disbursements** | Governance-approved spending rate projections |
| **Inflation** | New minting schedule (if applicable) |
| **Burns** | Revenue-based burn projections (bull/base/bear scenarios) |

### Model Outputs

- Monthly circulating supply trajectory
- Annual inflation/deflation rate
- Insider ownership % over time (declining as tokens vest)
- Treasury depletion timeline
- Token supply sensitivity to revenue scenarios (via burn impact)

{% hint style="success" %}
**Presentation Standard**

Present the model as a visual chart showing projected supply under **base, bull, and bear scenarios**. Token holders should clearly see when major supply events occur:
- Cliff unlocks (investor/team vesting cliffs)
- Emission halvings
- Treasury milestones
- Burn inflection points
{% endhint %}

## 12.5.2 Token Price Impact Simulation

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Regulatory Caution**

The investment banker should **never guarantee or project token prices** (regulatory and ethical prohibition). However, it is appropriate to model the relationship between protocol metrics and implied token value under different market multiple assumptions.
{% endhint %}

### Framework

At each point in the supply model, calculate:

```
Implied Price = (Protocol Revenue x Revenue Multiple) / Circulating Supply
```

### Worked Example (Base Case, 20x Revenue Multiple)

| Year | Revenue | Circulating Supply | Implied Price | Revenue Growth vs. Supply Growth |
|------|---------|-------------------|---------------|--------------------------------|
| 1 | $10M | 200M tokens | $1.00 | — |
| 2 | $25M | 350M tokens | $1.43 | Revenue +150%, Supply +75% |
| 3 | $50M | 500M tokens | $2.00 | Revenue +100%, Supply +43% |
| 5 | $150M | 800M tokens | $3.75 | Revenue +200%, Supply +60% |

**The critical insight:** Revenue growth must outpace supply growth for the token to appreciate. If revenue lags supply expansion, holders experience dilution pressure regardless of protocol quality.

This analysis should be run under multiple scenarios:
- **Bull:** Revenue grows faster than supply (value appreciation)
- **Base:** Revenue growth roughly matches supply growth (value stability)
- **Bear:** Revenue growth lags supply growth (dilution pressure)

## 12.5.3 Agent-Based Tokenomics Simulation

For complex tokenomic designs, agent-based simulation models test how different stakeholder behaviors affect token dynamics.

### Framework: cadCAD

cadCAD (complex adaptive dynamics Computer Aided Design), developed by BlockScience, enables simulation of multi-agent systems:

| Simulation Question | What It Reveals |
|--------------------|-----------------|
| How do rational stakers respond to changing yields? | Staking equilibrium and yield floor |
| What happens if all investor tokens are sold at the cliff? | Maximum sell pressure scenario |
| How does a 50% TVL decline affect staking economics? | Security degradation under stress |
| What is the impact of hostile governance accumulation? | Governance vulnerability thresholds |

{% hint style="info" %}
**Simulations Are Not Predictions**

Agent-based simulations are not predictive — they identify potential **failure modes** and stress-test parameters before they are implemented in immutable smart contracts. The goal is to discover edge cases and vulnerabilities, not to forecast prices.
{% endhint %}

### Key Stress Tests

1. **Cliff unlock stress test:** What happens to price and liquidity if 100% of cliff tokens are sold immediately?
2. **Bear market stress test:** If token price drops 80%, do staking yields remain attractive enough to maintain security?
3. **Governance attack stress test:** At what accumulation threshold can a hostile actor control governance?
4. **Incentive depletion stress test:** When do emission rewards become insufficient to attract users?
5. **Revenue collapse stress test:** If revenue drops 90%, how long can the treasury sustain operations?

---

## Key Takeaways

- The token supply model is a mandatory deliverable — project 5-10 years under multiple scenarios
- Price impact analysis reveals whether revenue growth outpaces supply expansion (the fundamental value question)
- Never guarantee or project token prices — model the relationships between metrics and implied value
- Agent-based simulation (cadCAD) identifies failure modes before deployment
- Key stress tests: cliff unlock, bear market, governance attack, incentive depletion, revenue collapse
- Present all models with visual charts showing major supply events and scenario comparisons
