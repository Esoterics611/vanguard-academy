# Part IV: Token Generation Events

> **A well-designed TGE creates a sustainable economic foundation that supports protocol growth for years. A poorly designed TGE can permanently impair a protocol's viability.**

---

## Overview

A Token Generation Event (TGE) is the decentralized equivalent of an Initial Public Offering. It simultaneously accomplishes what, in traditional finance, requires multiple distinct processes: capital formation, equity distribution, employee compensation design, incentive alignment, and public market listing.

This part of the handbook provides the **complete TGE advisory toolkit** — from readiness assessment and tokenomics design through distribution strategy, market making, exchange listings, and launch execution. The investment banker's role mirrors the IPO underwriter: designing the capital structure, managing distribution, coordinating liquidity, and ensuring a successful launch that serves the protocol's long-term interests.

{% hint style="success" %}
**The Stakes Are Extraordinary**

The history of crypto is littered with protocols that built excellent technology but destroyed their prospects through catastrophic token launches. A TGE is a one-shot event — there is no "re-IPO." The investment banker must get the tokenomics, distribution, and execution right the first time.
{% endhint %}
