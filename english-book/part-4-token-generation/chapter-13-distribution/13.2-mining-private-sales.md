# 13.2 Liquidity Mining and Private Sales

Liquidity mining provides the ongoing distribution that complements the one-time airdrop. Private sales fund protocol development and attract strategic partners before the TGE. Together with the airdrop, these three channels form the complete distribution strategy.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Learning Objectives**

After completing this section, you will be able to:
- Design a phased liquidity mining program with diminishing rewards
- Identify and mitigate mercenary capital risks
- Structure seed, strategic, and pre-TGE investment rounds
- Select investors based on strategic value, not just capital
{% endhint %}

---

## 13.2.1 Liquidity Mining Program Design

**Program Duration:** Defined phases (3-12 months each) with governance-approved renewals. Open-ended programs create permanent inflation pressure. Phased programs allow assessment and adjustment.

**Target Activities:**

| Activity | Typical Weight | Purpose |
|----------|---------------|---------|
| Liquidity provision | Highest | Bootstrap core protocol functionality |
| Active usage (borrow, trade) | High | Drive organic economic activity |
| Governance participation | Medium | Incentivize engaged governance |
| Referral | Low | Expand user base |

**Diminishing Returns Schedule:**

```
Phase 1 (Months 1-3):  HIGH rewards    → Bootstrap activity
Phase 2 (Months 4-6):  MEDIUM rewards  → Sustain growth
Phase 3 (Months 7-12): LOW rewards     → Transition to organic
Post-Phase 3:          MINIMAL/ZERO    → Protocol sustained by organic demand
```

### 13.2.2 The Mercenary Capital Problem

Mercenary capital: participants who provide liquidity solely to farm rewards and sell immediately, providing no lasting value. They inflate TVL during incentive periods, create token selling pressure, and withdraw completely when rewards end.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Mitigation Strategies**

| Strategy | How It Works | Effectiveness |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Lock-up requirements** | Mined tokens vest over 30-180 days | Most effective; reduces program attractiveness |
| **Boost mechanics** | Higher rewards for governance token holders | Filters pure mercenaries; ties mining to governance |
| **Retroactive distribution** | Rewards based on sustained participation | Full-period participants get full rewards; early leavers get less |
| **Minimum commitment** | 30-day minimum to qualify | Early withdrawal forfeits accumulated rewards |
{% endhint %}

## 13.3 Private Sales and Strategic Rounds

### 13.3.1 Pre-TGE Investor Rounds

Most protocols raise capital from institutional investors before the TGE. These rounds serve multiple purposes: funding development, attracting strategic partners, establishing a price reference, and building a base of sophisticated long-term holders.

**Round Structure:**

| Round | Stage | Typical FDV | Allocation | Vesting |
|-------|-------|-------------|------------|---------|
| **Seed** | Concept / prototype | $5-30M | 5-15% of supply | 4-year, 1-year cliff |
| **Strategic / Series A** | Post-launch, initial traction | $50-300M | 5-10% of supply | 3-4 year, 6-12 month cliff |
| **Pre-TGE** | Immediately before launch | 50-70% of expected TGE FDV | 2-5% of supply | 1-2 year, 3-6 month cliff |

### 13.3.2 Investor Selection Criteria

Not all capital is equal. Evaluate potential investors on:

**Strategic value:** Does the investor bring partnerships, technical expertise, or ecosystem connections beyond capital?

**Reputation:** Association with reputable investors (a16z crypto, Paradigm, Polychain, Framework Ventures) signals quality. Association with controversial investors creates risk.

**Holding behavior:** Analyze the investor's on-chain behavior from previous investments. Do they hold long-term or sell at the earliest opportunity?

**Alignment:** Is the investor philosophically aligned with the protocol's mission? Investors who pressure for short-term token price optimization at the expense of long-term health are destructive.

### 13.3.3 Valuation for Private Rounds

Private round valuation typically uses:

| Method | Approach |
|--------|----------|
| **Comparable analysis** | TGE FDV of comparable protocols, discounted for earlier stage |
| **Revenue/TVL multiples** | If protocol has launched, apply multiples with 30-50% illiquidity discount |
| **Milestone benchmarking** | Assess vs. milestones achieved by comparable protocols at similar funding stages |
| **Negotiation reality** | Supply/demand dynamics — hot protocols in bull markets command premiums |

{% hint style="info" %}
**The Illiquidity Discount**

Private round tokens are locked for 2-4 years. This illiquidity justifies a **30-50% discount** to the expected public market valuation. The discount compensates investors for the inability to sell during the lock-up period and the risk of adverse developments before vesting.
{% endhint %}

## Key Takeaways

- Liquidity mining programs must be phased (3-12 month intervals) with diminishing rewards
- Mercenary capital is the primary mining risk — mitigate with lock-ups, boosts, retroactive distribution, and minimums
- Three private round types (seed, strategic, pre-TGE) serve different purposes and carry different risk/return profiles
- Investor selection based on strategic value and alignment is as important as capital amount
- Apply 30-50% illiquidity discount for private round valuations relative to expected public market value
- Vesting requirements for investors should equal or exceed team vesting to signal alignment
