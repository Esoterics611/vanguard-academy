# Chapter 13: Distribution Strategy and Execution

Distribution strategy determines how tokens reach their intended holders — through airdrops, liquidity mining, and private sales. A well-designed distribution simultaneously rewards early users, creates broad ownership, generates awareness, and establishes market liquidity.

## Chapter Overview

This chapter covers the three distribution channels:

- **Airdrop design** — eligibility criteria, distribution formulas, anti-Sybil measures, and a complete case study
- **Liquidity mining** — program design, phased rewards, and mercenary capital mitigation
- **Private sales** — seed, strategic, and pre-TGE round structuring

## Key Concepts

{% hint style="info" %}
**The Distribution Principle**

The most effective token distributions reward genuine protocol usage rather than capital investment. This principle, established in the 2020-2021 airdrop era, remains the foundation of modern distribution strategy.
{% endhint %}
