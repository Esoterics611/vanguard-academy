# Chapter 15: Treasury Management as Institutional Asset Management

A DAO's treasury is its financial foundation -- the accumulated capital that funds operations, finances growth initiatives, provides security reserves, and enables strategic investment. Treasury management for DAOs is not a back-office function; it is a strategic discipline that directly determines the protocol's ability to execute its mission, weather adverse market conditions, and compete for talent and market share.

## Chapter Overview

This chapter provides the complete treasury management framework for DAO investment bankers:

- **The DAO treasury as a strategic asset** -- why treasury management demands institutional rigor combined with decentralized governance navigation
- **The Three-Bucket Treasury Model** -- organizing assets into Operating Reserve, Strategic Reserve, and Growth Portfolio with distinct risk/return/liquidity profiles
- **Native token position management** -- five strategies for converting theoretical treasury wealth into deployable capital
- **Real-world asset integration** -- tokenized T-bills, institutional credit, and money market funds
- **Risk management framework** -- six risk categories, the Treasury Risk Dashboard, and quarterly stress testing
- **Treasury governance and oversight** -- committee structure, reporting standards, and delegated authority
- **Case studies** -- Lido's OTC diversification and MakerDAO's four-phase treasury evolution

## Key Concepts

{% hint style="warning" %}
**The Treasury Paradox**

Most major DAO treasuries are simultaneously wealthy in notional terms and cash-poor in practical terms. The Arbitrum DAO held over $6 billion in ARB tokens at peak valuations, but the vast majority was illiquid. Selling even a small fraction would depress the token price, reducing the value of the remaining holdings -- a reflexive dynamic that makes the notional treasury value largely unrealizable.

The investment banker must help governance convert theoretical wealth into deployable capital without destroying value.
{% endhint %}
