## Background

In 2022, Lido DAO -- the largest liquid staking protocol -- faced a challenge common to nearly every major DAO: its treasury was overwhelmingly composed of its native LDO token. While the notional treasury value was substantial, the protocol's stablecoin reserves were insufficient to provide a multi-year operational runway in a bear market scenario.

The Lido DAO governance community recognized that proactive treasury diversification was necessary to ensure long-term operational sustainability. Rather than selling LDO on the open market -- which would create visible selling pressure and potentially depress the token price -- the DAO pursued an OTC transaction with an institutional investor.

---

## Transaction Structure

Lido DAO approved a treasury diversification proposal to sell approximately $50 million worth of LDO tokens to Dragonfly Capital through an OTC transaction.

**Key Terms:**

| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Token** | LDO (Lido governance token) |
| **Transaction Size** | ~$50 million notional |
| **Buyer** | Dragonfly Capital (institutional crypto VC) |
| **Pricing** | Discount to market price |
| **Lock-up** | Tokens subject to lock-up period |
| **Proceeds** | Stablecoins (treasury diversification) |

---

## The Controversy

The transaction was controversial within the Lido governance community. The debate centered on two opposing perspectives:

### Arguments Against

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Opposition Arguments**

- **Selling at a discount is unfair to existing holders** -- Why should an institutional investor receive LDO at a lower price than the market offers? This effectively transfers value from existing LDO holders to Dragonfly.
- **Timing concerns** -- Some members believed the DAO should wait for higher prices before diversifying, arguing that selling during a period of relative strength was premature.
- **Concentrated counterparty** -- Selling a large block to a single investor creates a concentrated holder with significant governance influence.
{% endhint %}

### Arguments For

{% hint style="success" %}
**Proponent Arguments**

- **Operational necessity** -- Without stablecoin reserves, the DAO would be unable to fund operations during a prolonged bear market. The cost of the discount is far less than the cost of insolvency.
- **No market impact** -- An OTC sale avoids the selling pressure that open-market diversification would create. The discount compensates the investor for lock-up illiquidity and concentration risk.
- **Strategic partnership** -- Dragonfly Capital brings institutional credibility, advisory capabilities, and ecosystem connections beyond just capital.
- **Bear market insurance** -- Diversification is most valuable before it is urgently needed. Waiting for "better prices" is a market-timing bet that could leave the DAO exposed.
{% endhint %}

---

## Outcome

The transaction ultimately proceeded through governance approval. The outcome validated the proponents' argument:

### Bear Market Validation

During the 2022 bear market, LDO's price declined over 80% from its peak. The stablecoin reserves obtained through the Dragonfly OTC transaction proved critical:

```
Illustrative Impact:

  Pre-diversification LDO treasury:    $500M notional (100% LDO)
  Post-80% decline:                    $100M notional (still 100% LDO, illiquid)

  Post-diversification treasury:       $450M LDO + $50M stablecoins
  Post-80% decline:                    $90M LDO + $50M stablecoins

  Operational impact:
  - Without diversification: Forced to sell LDO at distressed prices to fund operations
  - With diversification: $50M stablecoin runway regardless of LDO price
```

The $50 million in stablecoins maintained its value regardless of LDO's price decline. Without this diversification, Lido would have been forced to sell LDO tokens on the open market during the worst of the bear market -- at prices 80%+ lower than the OTC sale price, creating additional selling pressure and potentially threatening the protocol's viability.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**The Cost of Waiting**

If Lido had waited until the bear market to diversify, the same amount of operational capital would have required selling 5x more LDO tokens (given the 80% price decline). The OTC discount of 10-25% was a fraction of the "discount" the market would have imposed during a forced bear-market sale.

This is the treasury paradox in action: diversification feels expensive when markets are strong, but it is far more expensive when markets are weak.
{% endhint %}

---

## Key Lessons for Investment Bankers

### 1. Timing Principle

Treasury diversification is most valuable when done before it is urgently needed. The investment banker should proactively advocate for diversification during bull markets, when terms are favorable and the DAO has negotiating leverage.

### 2. Discount Justification

OTC discounts are not "giving away value." They are the fair market price for:
- Lock-up illiquidity
- Concentrated position risk
- Market risk during the lock-up period
- The operational certainty the proceeds provide

The investment banker should frame the discount analysis in terms of the cost of the alternative -- what would it cost the DAO to obtain the same capital through open-market sales or during a bear market?

### 3. Governance Navigation

Controversial treasury transactions require extensive governance preparation:
- Build the analytical case with clear data and scenario analysis
- Engage key delegates and large token holders before the formal vote
- Address counterarguments directly and honestly
- Frame the decision in terms of operational sustainability, not market timing

### 4. Counterparty Selection

The choice of OTC counterparty matters beyond the price:
- Institutional credibility enhances the signal value of the transaction
- Strategic capabilities (advisory, ecosystem connections) add non-financial value
- Governance engagement by the investor can strengthen the protocol's decision-making

{% hint style="success" %}
**Advisory Takeaway**

When advising a DAO on treasury diversification, the investment banker should present three scenarios:

1. **Diversify now** -- Cost: OTC discount (10-25%). Benefit: Operational certainty regardless of market conditions.
2. **Diversify later in favorable markets** -- Cost: Potentially lower discount. Risk: Markets may not remain favorable; opportunity cost of delay.
3. **Don't diversify** -- Cost: Zero upfront. Risk: Existential -- forced selling during bear markets at 50-80% lower prices.

Scenario analysis makes the case for proactive diversification far more compelling than abstract arguments about prudence.
{% endhint %}

---

## Case Study Takeaway

{% hint style="success" %}
**Key Analytical Takeaway**

Lido's OTC diversification demonstrates the cardinal principle of DAO treasury management: diversify before you need to. The ~10-25% OTC discount was a fraction of the 80%+ market decline that followed. The stablecoin reserves obtained through the transaction provided operational certainty that no amount of native tokens could have offered during the bear market. For the investment banker, this case study is the definitive argument for proactive treasury diversification advisory.
{% endhint %}
