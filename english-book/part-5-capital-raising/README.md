# Part V: Capital Raising for DAOs

> **Treasury diversification is most valuable when done before it is urgently needed. DAOs that diversify during bull markets preserve significantly more value than those forced to diversify during bear markets.**

---

## Overview

A DAO's treasury is its financial foundation — the accumulated capital that funds operations, finances growth, provides security reserves, and enables strategic investment. Yet most major DAO treasuries suffer from the "treasury paradox": simultaneously wealthy in notional terms and cash-poor in practical terms.

This part of the handbook provides the **complete capital raising and treasury management toolkit** — from institutional-grade asset allocation frameworks through capital raising instrument design, investor relations, cap table management, and fundraising execution.
