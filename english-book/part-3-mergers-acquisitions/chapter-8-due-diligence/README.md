# Chapter 8: Due Diligence for DAO Acquisitions

Due diligence is the analytical process through which the acquirer develops a comprehensive understanding of the target's value, risks, and integration challenges. DAO due diligence is simultaneously more transparent and more technically complex than traditional due diligence. More transparent because much of the information is publicly available on-chain. More complex because evaluating smart contract risk, tokenomic dynamics, governance health, and decentralized contributor networks requires specialized expertise that does not exist in traditional M&A advisory.

## Chapter Overview

This chapter provides the complete due diligence framework for DAO acquisitions, organized into **ten specialized workstreams**:

1. Smart Contract and Technical DD
2. Tokenomic DD
3. Financial DD
4. Governance DD
5. Legal and Regulatory DD
6. Team and Contributor DD
7. Competitive and Market DD
8. On-Chain Forensic Analysis
9. Community and Sentiment DD
10. Integration Risk Assessment

## Key Concepts

{% hint style="danger" %}
**The Fei/Rari Lesson**

The Fei Protocol/Rari Capital merger was devastated by an $80 million exploit of Rari's Fuse lending pools. Had the acquirer's due diligence included a line-by-line comparison of Rari's modified code against the original Compound V2 codebase, the reentrancy vulnerability might have been identified. Smart contract DD is not optional — it is the single most critical workstream.
{% endhint %}
