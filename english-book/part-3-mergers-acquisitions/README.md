# Part III: Mergers and Acquisitions of DAOs

> **The investment banker who develops expertise in DAO M&A now will be positioned at the center of a transaction market that will expand by orders of magnitude over the coming decade.**

---

## Overview

Mergers and acquisitions represent the most complex, highest-stakes engagements in investment banking. In the DAO ecosystem, M&A is nascent but growing rapidly as protocols mature, competitive pressures intensify, and treasuries accumulate sufficient capital to fund acquisitions.

This part of the handbook provides the **complete DAO M&A toolkit** — from strategic rationale and target identification through due diligence, transaction structuring, and post-merger integration. The frameworks adapt Wall Street M&A methodology to the unique realities of decentralized governance, open-source technology, and token-based economics.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Governance Changes Everything**

The fundamental difference between traditional M&A and DAO M&A is governance. Every transaction requires approval from decentralized communities, every integration decision is subject to governance debate, and disaffected participants can fork the protocol. The investment banker who masters governance dynamics masters DAO M&A.
{% endhint %}
