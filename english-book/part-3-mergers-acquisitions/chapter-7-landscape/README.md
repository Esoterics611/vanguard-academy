# Chapter 7: The Landscape of DAO M&A

In traditional corporate finance, mergers and acquisitions are driven by a well-understood set of strategic rationales. These same motivations exist in the DAO ecosystem, but they manifest in unique ways shaped by decentralized organizational structure, token-based economics, and the open-source technology paradigm.

## Chapter Overview

This chapter maps the DAO M&A landscape. You will learn:

- **Why DAOs merge and acquire** — the seven strategic rationales and how they differ from traditional M&A
- **The taxonomy of transaction types** — from friendly DAO-to-DAO mergers to hostile governance takeovers
- **Market scale and trajectory** — how DAO M&A is evolving from dozens of deals to a multi-billion dollar market
- **The complete M&A process** — an eight-phase timeline adapted for governance-based decision-making

## Key Concepts

{% hint style="info" %}
**The Non-Forkable Premium**

In DAO M&A, the acquisition premium over the cost of forking represents the value of non-forkable assets: the development team's institutional knowledge, the user base and liquidity, brand equity, integration networks, and the governance community. This is the DAO-native equivalent of goodwill.
{% endhint %}
