# 4.2 Building a Complete DCF Model: Aave Protocol Case Study

Let us build a complete DCF model for Aave, one of the largest lending protocols in DeFi. This case study demonstrates the full methodology from historical analysis through to per-token valuation.

## Step 1: Historical Financial Analysis

Aave's historical financials (approximate, sourced from Token Terminal and DefiLlama):

### Year 2021
- **TVL:** $15-30 billion (peak)
- **Total borrows:** $8-15 billion
- **Annualized protocol revenue:** ~$150-200 million
- **Deployment:** Ethereum, Polygon, Avalanche, Arbitrum, Optimism

### Year 2022
- **TVL:** $5-10 billion (bear market contraction)
- **Total borrows:** $3-6 billion
- **Annualized protocol revenue:** ~$50-80 million
- **Context:** Significant contraction due to market downturn, UST/Luna collapse, contagion effects

### Year 2023
- **TVL:** $8-12 billion (recovery)
- **Total borrows:** $5-8 billion
- **Annualized protocol revenue:** ~$60-100 million
- **Development:** Aave V3 deployment driving capital efficiency improvements

### Year 2024
- **TVL:** $15-20 billion (bull market recovery)
- **Total borrows:** $8-12 billion
- **Annualized protocol revenue:** ~$150-250 million
- **New products:** GHO stablecoin launch providing additional revenue stream
- **Expansion:** Aave deployed on 10+ chains

{% hint style="info" %}
**Key Observation**

Aave's revenue is highly cyclical, correlating strongly with overall crypto market conditions and leverage demand. This cyclicality must be reflected in our projections.
{% endhint %}

## Step 2: Revenue Projections (5-Year Explicit Forecast)

Our projection model requires assumptions about:
1. Total crypto lending market size
2. Aave's market share
3. Net protocol revenue as a percentage of total borrows

### Base Case Assumptions

| Assumption | Rationale |
|------------|-----------|
| **Total DeFi lending market:** $30B → $150B over 5 years (37% CAGR) | Driven by institutional adoption, real-world asset integration, broader crypto market growth |
| **Aave market share:** 35-40% | Currently the dominant lending protocol |
| **Net interest margin to protocol:** 1.0-1.5% of total borrows | Aave retains ~15-25% of interest spreads |
| **GHO stablecoin:** Grows to $5B outstanding, generating ~3% annual revenue | New revenue stream from stablecoin operations |

### Year 1 Projection

```
Aave total borrows: $15 billion (35% of $43B market)
Core lending revenue: $15B × 1.2% = $180 million
GHO revenue: $1.5B outstanding × 3% = $45 million
Total revenue: $225 million
```

### Year 2 Projection

```
Aave total borrows: $21 billion (36% of $58B market)
Core lending revenue: $21B × 1.2% = $252 million
GHO revenue: $2.5B × 3% = $75 million
Total revenue: $327 million
```

### Year 3 Projection

```
Aave total borrows: $28 billion (37% of $76B market)
Core lending revenue: $28B × 1.25% = $350 million
GHO revenue: $3.5B × 3% = $105 million
Total revenue: $455 million
```

### Year 4 Projection

```
Aave total borrows: $37 billion (37% of $100B market)
Core lending revenue: $37B × 1.25% = $463 million
GHO revenue: $4.5B × 3% = $135 million
Total revenue: $598 million
```

### Year 5 Projection

```
Aave total borrows: $52 billion (35% of $150B market, slight share erosion)
Core lending revenue: $52B × 1.2% = $624 million
GHO revenue: $5.0B × 3% = $150 million
Total revenue: $774 million
```

**Revenue CAGR:** ~28% over the forecast period

## Step 3: Expense Projections

Aave's operating expenses are estimated based on governance proposal disclosures and observable treasury outflows:

| Expense Category | Annual Amount | Growth Rate |
|------------------|---------------|-------------|
| **Core contributor compensation** | $25-35M annually | 15% per year as protocol scales |
| **Security and audits** | $5-8M annually | Stable |
| **Infrastructure and oracle costs** | $3-5M annually | Stable |
| **Ecosystem grants and incentives** | $15-25M annually | Declining as % of revenue as protocol matures |
| **Legal and compliance** | $3-5M annually | Growing with regulatory requirements |
| **GHO-specific operations** | $5-10M annually | New |

**Total operating expenses:** Year 1 ~$65 million, scaling to Year 5 ~$110 million

## Step 4: Protocol Free Cash Flow Calculation

| Year | Revenue | Expenses | Dev CapEx | Reserve | **PFCF** |
|------|---------|----------|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Year 1** | $225M | $65M | $15M | $10M | **$135M** |
| **Year 2** | $327M | $75M | $18M | $12M | **$222M** |
| **Year 3** | $455M | $85M | $20M | $15M | **$335M** |
| **Year 4** | $598M | $95M | $22M | $18M | **$463M** |
| **Year 5** | $774M | $110M | $25M | $20M | **$619M** |

## Step 5: Terminal Value

Using a perpetuity growth model with a terminal growth rate of 3% (reflecting long-run nominal GDP growth as an upper bound for sustainable protocol growth):

```
Terminal Value = Year 5 PFCF × (1 + g) / (r - g)

Where:
g = 3% (terminal growth rate)
r = 30% (cost of equity for mature, battle-tested DeFi protocol)

Terminal Value = $619M × 1.03 / (0.30 - 0.03)
               = $637.6M / 0.27
               = $2,361 million
```

## Step 6: Present Value Calculation

**Discount rate:** 30%

| Year | PFCF | Discount Factor | Present Value |
|------|------|-----------------|---------------|
| Year 1 | $135M | 1/(1.30)^1 | **$103.8M** |
| Year 2 | $222M | 1/(1.30)^2 | **$131.4M** |
| Year 3 | $335M | 1/(1.30)^3 | **$152.5M** |
| Year 4 | $463M | 1/(1.30)^4 | **$162.1M** |
| Year 5 | $619M | 1/(1.30)^5 | **$166.7M** |
| Terminal Value | $2,361M | 1/(1.30)^5 | **$635.6M** |

**Total Protocol Value** = $103.8M + $131.4M + $152.5M + $162.1M + $166.7M + $635.6M = **$1,352.1 million**

## Step 7: Per-Token Value

Aave has a total supply of **16 million AAVE tokens**. Assuming full dilution:

```
DCF Value per AAVE = $1,352.1M / 16M = $84.51
```

### Treasury Adjustment

The Aave DAO treasury holds approximately $100-200 million in non-AAVE assets (stablecoins, ETH, protocol-owned liquidity). Adding $150M in net treasury value:

```
Adjusted Protocol Value = $1,352.1M + $150M = $1,502.1M
Adjusted DCF Value per AAVE = $1,502.1M / 16M = $93.88
```

## Step 8: Sensitivity Analysis

The table below shows per-token value under different discount rate and terminal growth rate assumptions:

| Discount Rate | Terminal Growth 2% | Terminal Growth 3% | Terminal Growth 4% |
|---------------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **25%** | $137.45 | $152.18 | $170.63 |
| **30%** | $88.21 | $93.88 | $100.76 |
| **35%** | $63.14 | $66.02 | $69.34 |
| **40%** | $48.55 | $50.21 | $52.12 |

### Key Observations

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Extreme Sensitivity to Discount Rate**

The value range of **$50-$170 per AAVE** demonstrates the enormous sensitivity to discount rate assumptions, reinforcing why cost of capital estimation is the single most important judgment in protocol valuation.
{% endhint %}

- Terminal growth rate has a more modest impact because the high discount rate diminishes the present value of distant cash flows
- The model implies that at a market price of approximately $100-150 per AAVE (mid-2024 range), the market is pricing in assumptions roughly consistent with our base case at a 25-30% discount rate
- This suggests the market views Aave as a relatively established, lower-risk DeFi protocol

## Step 9: Scenario Analysis

### Bull Case (30% probability)
- **Assumptions:** DeFi lending market grows to $300B, Aave maintains 40% share, regulatory clarity accelerates institutional adoption
- **Year 5 PFCF:** $1.4 billion
- **Protocol value:** $3.5 billion
- **Per-token:** **$219**

### Base Case (50% probability)
- **Assumptions:** As modeled above
- **Protocol value:** $1.5 billion
- **Per-token:** **$94**

### Bear Case (20% probability)
- **Assumptions:** Regulatory crackdown limits DeFi lending, smart contract exploit impairs confidence, competitors erode market share
- **Year 5 PFCF:** $150 million
- **Protocol value:** $500 million
- **Per-token:** **$31**

### Probability-Weighted Expected Value

```
Expected Value = (0.30 × $219) + (0.50 × $94) + (0.20 × $31)
               = $65.7 + $47.0 + $6.2
               = $118.90 per AAVE
```

## Step 10: Dilution Adjustment

The per-token DCF value calculated above uses fully diluted token supply. However, ongoing token-based compensation creates continuous dilution that must be modeled.

### Token Emissions vs. Buybacks

If Aave distributes **1% of total supply annually** as contributor compensation (160,000 AAVE per year), the cumulative dilution over the 5-year forecast period is approximately 5%, reducing per-token value proportionally.

### Buyback Mechanism

Aave has implemented a token buyback mechanism using protocol revenue (the "Buy & Distribute" program approved in governance).

**Calculation:**
- If the protocol uses **10% of revenue** to buy back AAVE tokens
- Annual buybacks: approximately $22.5 million in Year 1, growing with revenue
- At $100 per AAVE, this retires approximately **225,000 tokens per year**
- This potentially **exceeds token emissions**, making AAVE net deflationary

{% hint style="success" %}
**Net Supply Impact**

The net supply change must be projected year by year and incorporated into the per-token value calculation. If buybacks exceed emissions, the per-token value increases over time even if total protocol value remains constant.
{% endhint %}
