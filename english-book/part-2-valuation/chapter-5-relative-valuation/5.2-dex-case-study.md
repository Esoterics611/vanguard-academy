# 5.2 Case Study: Comparative Valuation of DEX Protocols

Let us construct a complete comparable analysis for valuing a hypothetical mid-tier DEX protocol, **"Protocol X,"** which generates $30 million in annualized revenue, has $500 million in TVL, and processes $15 billion in annual trading volume.

## 5.2.1 Peer Group Construction

### Uniswap

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Market Cap (FDV) | ~$8 billion |
| Revenue (annualized) | ~$500 million (gross to LPs) |
| Revenue to protocol | $0 (fee switch off) |
| Potential protocol revenue | $50-100M (with fee switch) |
| TVL | ~$5 billion |
| Volume (annualized) | ~$500 billion |
| P/R multiple | N/A (no protocol revenue) |

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Uniswap Valuation Complexity**

Uniswap's valuation entirely reflects **governance/optionality value** since no revenue accrues to token holders. This makes Uniswap a poor direct comp for revenue-based multiples but an important reference for governance premium analysis.

UNI trades at a substantial premium despite zero revenue capture—the market is pricing the **option value** of the fee switch.
{% endhint %}

## 5.2.2 Peer Group Analysis

### Multiple Range Summary

| Metric | Range | Median |
|--------|-------|--------|
| P/R Multiple | 15-50x | 20-25x |
| Revenue/TVL | 1.5-6% | 3-4% |
| Market Cap/TVL | 0.3-0.8x | 0.5x |

The wide P/R range (15-50x) reflects differences in:
- Growth expectations
- Competitive positioning
- Ecosystem quality
- Token economics design

{% hint style="info" %}
**Multiple Selection Drivers**

**High-end multiples (30-50x):** Reserved for protocols with:
- Dominant market position
- Strong network effects
- Sustainable competitive advantages
- High growth trajectory

**Low-end multiples (15-20x):** Applied to protocols with:
- Execution challenges
- Competitive threats
- Mature/declining growth
- Single-chain concentration risk
{% endhint %}

## 5.2.3 Applying Multiples to Protocol X

### Protocol X Baseline Metrics

```
Revenue (annualized):    $30 million
TVL:                     $500 million
Volume (annualized):     $15 billion
Revenue/TVL:             6% (high efficiency)
```

### Valuation Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Low Multiple (15x)**
```
Valuation = $30M × 15 = $450 million
```
**Justification:** Mature protocol with stable but not growing market share

**Scenario 2: Mid-Low Multiple (20x)**
```
Valuation = $30M × 20 = $600 million
```
**Justification:** Solid competitive positioning, moderate growth prospects

**Scenario 3: Mid-High Multiple (25x)**
```
Valuation = $30M × 25 = $750 million
```
**Justification:** Strong growth trajectory, improving market share

**Scenario 4: High Multiple (30x)**
```
Valuation = $30M × 30 = $900 million
```
**Justification:** Exceptional competitive positioning, rapid growth, strong innovation pipeline

### Multiple Selection Framework

Multiple selection requires qualitative judgment based on:

#### 1. Competitive Positioning
- **Strong:** Premium multiple (25-30x)
- **Stable:** Mid-range (20-25x)
- **Weak:** Discount multiple (15-20x)

#### 2. Growth Trajectory
- **Accelerating growth:** +5-10 points
- **Stable growth:** Base multiple
- **Declining growth:** -5-10 points

#### 3. Capital Efficiency
Protocol X has 6% Revenue/TVL—**significantly higher** than peer median of 3-4%. This suggests:
- Superior monetization
- Efficient capital utilization
- Justifies premium to peer group median

#### 4. Risk Factors
Consider:
- Smart contract risk exposure
- Chain concentration risk
- Regulatory exposure
- Team execution capability

## 5.2.4 Final Valuation Range

### Base Comparable Valuation

**Implied valuation range:** $450 million to $900 million

**Central estimate:** $600-750 million (20-25x multiple)

{% hint style="success" %}
**Capital Efficiency Premium**

Protocol X's superior Revenue/TVL ratio (6% vs. peer median of 3-4%) justifies a valuation toward the **higher end of the range** ($700-750 million).

This assumes Protocol X maintains its capital efficiency advantage through:
- Superior product design
- Better user experience
- More favorable fee structure
- Strategic market positioning
{% endhint %}

### Adjustments to Base Valuation

After establishing base valuation, consider:

1. **Growth Premium/Discount:** ±15-25% for every 10pp growth differential
2. **Treasury Premium:** Add excess treasury value (excluding native tokens)
3. **Risk Discount:** -10-30% for elevated smart contract or governance risk
4. **Liquidity Discount:** -10-25% for lower token liquidity vs. peers
5. **Control Premium:** +20-40% if valuing for acquisition context

## Key Takeaways

1. **Multiple ranges are wide** - DEX protocols trade at 15-50x revenue depending on positioning
2. **Capital efficiency drives premium** - Higher Revenue/TVL justifies higher multiples
3. **Qualitative factors matter** - Growth, competitive position, and risk profile determine multiple selection
4. **Central tendency is 20-25x** - For established DEXs with stable market share
5. **Option value adds premium** - Protocols with fee switch options (like Uniswap) trade above revenue-based fundamentals
